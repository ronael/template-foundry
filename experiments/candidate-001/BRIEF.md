# Candidate 001 — Kern

## Product

Kern is a fictional reliability platform for teams shipping production AI agents. It records complete traces, turns failures into reusable evaluations, and gives engineering teams evidence for release decisions.

- Target customer: AI platform, product, and reliability teams at technical SaaS companies.
- Positioning: the operating record between agent experimentation and dependable production.
- Tone: exact, calm, technical, confident; never mystical or breathless.
- Format: responsive React/Vite web prototype. It is not a native Framer project yet.

## Direction

“Field manual for reliable agents”: editorial utility, visible structure, restrained color, and real product evidence. Warm paper, near-black ink, one acid-chartreuse action color, compact mono annotations, low radii, and dense but legible operational UI. The identity deliberately avoids purple gradients, glass cards, AI orbs, and decorative bento grids.

## Pages and narrative

- Home: problem → reliability workflow → failure analysis → collaboration → proof → pricing → action.
- Product: trace record → evaluations → monitoring → SDK → security.
- Pricing: three plans → capability comparison → objections → action.

## Design system

- Color: paper `#f4f3ed`, ink `#141510`, accent `#c9f500`, red `#b82e28`.
- Type: Archivo Variable for editorial hierarchy; IBM Plex Mono for evidence and controls.
- Spacing: 4/8/12/18/24/32/48/72/96 rhythm.
- Shape: 0–6px radii, one-pixel rules, minimal shadow reserved for the primary product view.
- Components: masthead, action link, numbered rail, trace viewer, evidence panels, pricing cards, footer.
- Motion profile: `minimal`. Navigation compresses on scroll, exposes reading progress and indexed route states, and transitions the mobile menu. Short positional entrances and state transitions support the product; no essential content depends on animation.
- Responsive: composition changes from editorial rail/grid to a single reading column; product evidence remains legible and interactive.
